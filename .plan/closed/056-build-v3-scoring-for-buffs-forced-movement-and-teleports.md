## Resolution

Built on branch `ticket-056-build-v3-scoring-for-buffs-and-forced-movement`.

- Buffs. `tactics::BUFF_HORIZON = 2` and `tactics::SAVE_ADVANTAGE_VALUE = 0.3` are public beside `AC_POINT_VALUE`, which prices AC +n and attack-roll +n. An ability whose top-level effects (read through a cast's bound effects) are all timed modifiers gets a `modifier_plan`: `self` modifiers are valued on the actor, and `target` ones on the actor and each living ally, best wins, ties to the actor and then the lower id. The plan targets that bearer and its reason reads `haste on standard-striker-11 (+2 AC against 51.5 attack damage, advantage on dex saves against 0.0 dex save damage, extra action 21.1)`. A bearer that already has a modifier from the same `source_ability` scores 0 for it. Modifiers under `on_failure` or `on_hit` stay in the zero arm with `TempHp`, `RollModifier` and `Teleport`. `self` modifiers on an ability that also attacks add to that plan's score.
- Extra action. An `extra_action` modifier adds the bearer's best expected damage from one action `engine::extra_action_allows` accepts, against its top-priority opponent and capped at its HP, per round of the horizon, and nothing while the bearer can't act. `Section::Extra` ("extra action" in logs) makes `choose` return `None` unless `engine::extra_action_uses` has uses, and offers only allowed actions. `batch::take_turn` runs it as a third section after action and bonus action, so a failed `execute` goes through `Log::plan_failed` with `"extra action"`, is retried once, and is marked lost when nothing ran; `execute` sends `extra: true`.
- Forced movement. `forced_move_factor`: a pull, a push under 5 feet, or a target with any attack in its actions or bonus actions that doesn't need engagement scores 0; a push of 35 feet or more on a target whose `walk_speed` is under 35 scores 1.0; any other push 0.25; times the target's threat as control, guarded like conditions by `MAX_THREAT_NESTING`.
- Docs. ADR 0012 gains a paragraph for buffs, extra actions and forced movement. The batch report's method sentence now says the policy "values an effect that raises AC, saving throws or attack rolls by the damage it prevents or adds over two rounds on the ally it helps most, Haste's extra action as one more attack, and a push as the melee it denies, gives teleports and other unscored effects no value". CHANGELOG has an entry under Changed.
- Tests. - Audit fixes. A bearer that can't act gets no extra-action value (its AC and save parts still count). AC is priced against the attack-roll damage on the bearer and save Advantage against damage from saves of the modifier's ability: `incoming_attack_rolls` and `attack_roll_value` became `incoming_rolls` and `roll_value` over `Rolls::Attacks(filter)` or `Rolls::Saves(ability)`, so the reaction reserve and the buff value share one walk; a save leaf also looks inside an area, and the attack walk is unchanged. `forced_move_factor` reads bonus actions too.

Readings chosen where the ticket was open:

- Incoming damage for a bearer is bearer-specific and split by roll: attack-roll damage for AC, damage from saves of the matching ability for save Advantage, each opponent using its best ready action for those rolls. The existing `incoming` averages each opponent's threat over every foe, so it is the same for every ally, and it mixes saves into what AC stops. Heals and consumables still read the old `incoming`. A save carried in an attack's `on_hit` is not counted as save damage.
- `breaks_own_concentration` also holds when any active modifier has the actor as `source` with a concentration duration. Without it, the caster recast Haste onto a second ally the next turn, ending the first Haste and lethargizing that ally.
- Advantage on one ability's saves (Haste's Dexterity) counts 0.3 of the damage from that ability's saves only, and the reason names the ability. "Disadvantage on foes' attacks" has no modifier target today (`attack_rolls` is the bearer's own), so nothing scores it.
- Horizon: `rounds(n)` gives min(n, 2), `until_end_of_next_turn` 1, `instant`, `triggering_attack` and `until_end_of_turn` 0, everything else 2.
- Forced movement: both tiers need a target whose every attack needs engagement, since 045's "ranged-only" zero would otherwise leave a target with a bow and a sword pushed for full value.
- An extra action with an empty `only` list can take any action plan, not only a single attack, because the engine allows it.
- A scratch batch run from the repo's debug build wrote `~/.bench/reports/ogre-seed-7/` in the real home folder before the scratch `HOME` was set; it is a stray report and was left in place.
