## Question

Where does the encounter's lighting come from, and how is it set? Roll modifiers name a `Lighting` value (`bench-core/src/effect.rs`), but no live command sets one. Decide whether lighting is one value per encounter or per combatant, its default, the flag on `bench start` and `bench batch`, whether `bench override` can change it mid-fight, and how the batch report states it as an assumption.

## Notes

ADR 0005 introduced roll modifiers with a `lighting` setting. Darkvision and Sunlight Sensitivity are the common SRD cases. Consult `grilling` and `domain-modeling`.

## Resolution

Decided on 2026-11-04 by a delegated agent, under the user's instruction to make the decision without coming back. Evidence: ADR 0003, 0005, 0008 and 0011, closed tickets 013, 041, 042 and 054, `bench-core/src/effect.rs`, `engine.rs`, `batch.rs`, `encounter.rs`, `override_path.rs`, the fixtures, and PHB "Vision and Light" (ch. 1, p. 18), the rules glossary entries Bright Light, Dim Light, Darkness, Heavily Obscured and Darkvision (pp. 361 to 367), and Daylight (ch. 7, p. 259).

What the code does today: `Lighting` is `normal`, `sunlight` or `bright_light`, and only `RollModifier` names it. The engine treats a `RollModifier` as an action effect that pushes a concentration modifier on the actor and never reads `lighting`. Nothing sets a lighting value. No fixture uses `roll_modifier`. Eleven fixtures carry a lighting trait, all imported as unmodeled with the `skipped` default: Sunlight Sensitivity on the kobold warrior, drider, specter, wight and wraith (Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls), Sunlight Weakness on the shadow (Disadvantage on D20 Tests), the Sunlight trait on the vampire spawn and the three vampire forms (the same Disadvantage plus 20 Radiant damage at the start of a turn in sunlight), and Light Sensitivity on the cloaker (Disadvantage on attack rolls in Bright Light). Saving throws never roll with Advantage or Disadvantage. The engine rolls no ability checks.

- **One value per encounter.** `EncounterSettings` gains `lighting`, fixed at `bench start` like `pc_dice`. Reason: ADR 0003 has no positions, so the engine can't say which combatant stands in which light. Ticket 013 already named it an encounter setting.
- **Values: `unspecified`, `dim_light`, `bright_light`, `sunlight`.** `normal` is renamed `unspecified`, and `dim_light` is new. Reason: the PHB glossary says "Bright Light is normal illumination", so `normal` beside `bright_light` named the same thing twice. `unspecified` says the user stated no lighting; the other three match the rules terms.
- **Sunlight counts as Bright Light.** A roll modifier keyed to `bright_light` applies under `bright_light` or `sunlight`. One keyed to `sunlight` applies only under `sunlight`, and one keyed to `dim_light` only under `dim_light`. `unspecified` meets no key, and no roll modifier may be keyed to it. Reason: Daylight's sunlight "is Bright Light", and the PHB gives Bright Light to even gloomy days, so sunlight is the narrower case.
- **Default `unspecified`.** It turns on no lighting trait. Reason: any real light level would switch on some trait in every default batch. `bright_light` would give the cloaker Disadvantage in every fight, and `dim_light` would switch on homebrew "while in Dim Light or Darkness" traits. A neutral value adds no lighting the user didn't choose, so every fixture's batch numbers stay as they are today.
- **Roll modifiers are trait effects read at roll time.** The engine checks the roller's traits for `roll_modifier` effects whose `lighting` the encounter meets, and applies their disposition to the matching roll. A `roll_modifier` in an action, bonus action, reaction or legendary action is refused when the stat block is validated, so a bad homebrew file fails at load and not mid-fight, and the push-a-concentration-modifier branch goes. Reason: every SRD case is a standing trait, and the `no_provoke` effect (ADR 0015) already set the pattern of the engine reading trait effects. ADR 0005's other roll-modifier conditions (an ally within 5 feet, Bloodied and the rest) stay unbuilt and are not decided here.
- **Rolls it reaches.** `attack_rolls` goes through the existing attack disposition. `saving_throws` gains a disposition: worked out from two flags, any Advantage and any Disadvantage, so that one of each gives a straight roll however many sources there are (PHB glossary "Advantage", p. 359). A save with Advantage or Disadvantage draws a second d20, and only then, so saves under the default draw the same dice as before. In ask mode the second d20 prompts the player like the attack's second die. `ability_checks` is stored and shown, but the engine rolls no checks, so it changes nothing yet. Reason: Sunlight Weakness names D20 Tests, and saves are the one D20 Test the engine rolls that it can't modify today.
- **Each use shows in the log.** An attack or save whose disposition came from a lighting roll modifier says so in its event, naming the trait, for example `(Disadvantage: Sunlight Sensitivity, sunlight)`. Reason: ADR 0006 logs everything the engine does, and a reader must see why a kobold rolled twice.
- **SRD import.** Three exact sentence shapes become roll modifiers: "While in sunlight, the <creature> has Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls." (also with "it" and the two kinds swapped), "While in sunlight, the <creature> has Disadvantage on D20 Tests." (attack rolls, saving throws and ability checks) and "While in Bright Light, the <creature> has Disadvantage on attack rolls." Each writes a class (d) review note, recorded as a third exception in ADR 0008. The vampire Sunlight trait becomes partly modeled: the two modifiers run, the 20 Radiant damage stays an agent clause, and the trait keeps its `skipped` default, so `ignored_abilities` on the curve stays as it is. The cloaker's Phantasms line about Bright Light stays in its unmodeled text. Reason: ticket 058 set the exact-sentence exception for traits upstream has no field for, and these eight words repeat across 11 fixtures.
- **Flags.** `bench start --lighting <unspecified|dim_light|bright_light|sunlight>` and `bench batch --lighting <...>`, default `unspecified`, spelled in snake_case so the flag, the state and the override path use one spelling. Reason: lighting is an encounter input like `--pc-dice` or `--party`, not an ability resolution, and `--assume` paths take only `policy.*` and `abilities.*`.
- **Override mid-fight.** `bench override settings.lighting <value> --reason <text>` changes it, logged and undoable like any override. Nothing new is needed beyond the field: `settings` is already overridable and public. Reason: Daylight, a fight moving outdoors, or dawn are real mid-fight changes, and ADR 0006 already covers them.
- **Live visibility.** The digest and the public digest carry `lighting`, and the log's setup lines name it. Reason: players can see the light, and the agent needs it to narrate and to judge what the engine doesn't model.
- **Batch report.** The Standing assumptions table gains a fixed Lighting row, for example "Lighting: unspecified, lighting traits off". The sample logs' STANDING ASSUMPTIONS block prints `lighting = <value>`, the JSON summary carries `lighting`, and each sample's replay command adds `--lighting` when it isn't the default. A modeled trait whose only effects are lighting roll modifiers gets an ability-resolution line saying whether this run's lighting turned it on. When the lighting isn't `unspecified`, the basis line says the curve's fixtures fought with lighting unspecified. Reason: ticket 013 called lighting a standing assumption, ticket 017 requires every input stated, and ticket 042 requires replays to rerun exactly.
- **Baseline.** The curve is generated with lighting `unspecified`, written into the generator and noted in ADR 0011. The curve gets no lighting field. Reason: it is a fixed reference, and a run in another light compares against it on purpose.
- **Batch policy.** The tactics policy doesn't read lighting, and `POLICY_VERSION` stays at 2. Reason: Disadvantage from these traits hits all of a creature's attacks alike, so it seldom changes the best plan, and 056 owns the next policy change.
- **Versions and baseline.** `ENGINE_VERSION` doesn't move, because batch numbers under the default are unchanged: the attack already draws two d20s, saves draw a second die only when the disposition isn't straight, and every lighting trait is off when the lighting is unspecified. The build proves this on the 11 fixtures. The fixture set fingerprint changes, a stale reason the tests already accept, so the build lands before ticket 056's one regeneration, and 056 now waits on it. The `Lighting` rename keeps `schema_version` at 1 by recorded exception, because no committed stat block uses the old value, and `normal` stays a serde alias of `unspecified` so old homebrew and state files still load.

Left out:

- Darkness and Darkvision. In Darkness a creature without Darkvision, Blindsight or Truesight is Blinded. One encounter-wide `darkness` value read against each creature's senses needs no positions, so it moves to Not yet specified in `map.md`. Vision that depends on position (sense ranges against range bands, who sees whom per pair) stays Out of scope.
- Light shed by creatures (the fire elemental, the will-o'-wisp), the Darkness and Daylight spells as areas, and one combatant standing in different light from the rest. The agent overrides `settings.lighting` or judges it.
- Start-of-turn damage in sunlight (vampires) stays an agent clause with its `skipped` default.
- Dim Light's Perception penalty stays unmodeled because the engine rolls no Perception checks.

- The engine's attack disposition chains `improve` and `worsen`, so with several sources the result depends on order, against the PHB rule that any Advantage plus any Disadvantage is a straight roll. The tactics policy's `disposition` sums and clamps with the same fault. Fixing attacks changes batch numbers, so it is its own build ticket.
