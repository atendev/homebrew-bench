# Map: homebrew-bench

## Destination

A repo someone downloads to test D&D 5e homebrew monsters by running combats and reading the numbers, aimed at DMs first and homebrew designers second. One engine holds combat state and does the rules math. An agent-agnostic skill teaches any LLM how to use it, in two modes:

- Batch mode: the agent converts pasted monster text to a structured form, flags ambiguous wording, runs many fights, and writes a report with win rate, rounds, and damage per round across party levels, compared with CR guidance.
- Live mode: the agent DMs one full combat encounter turn by turn through the engine while the user controls the party.

Both modes ship in phase 1. Everything the engine can't model is labeled agent judgment. Every engine action goes into a human-readable log.

## Not yet specified

- Dash as a movement budget: what a doubled move is worth with range bands, for Cunning Action, Deathless Agility and Hasten ([Bonus-action Disengage](closed/090-bonus-action-disengage.md)).
- Hide: a hidden state from the Hide action, beside how creatures see Invisible ones ([Seeing Invisible creatures by spell or trait](closed/095-seeing-invisible-creatures.md)).
- Invisible monsters in batch: no carrier puts Invisible on a monster in batch (self-cast Invisibility and the invisible stalker's trait are unmodeled), and nobody has decided whether the party answers it with See Invisibility or Faerie Fire or the report only shows the swing ([Seeing Invisible creatures by spell or trait](closed/095-seeing-invisible-creatures.md)).
- Prowl: a move without provoking as one bonus action. It may fall out of Dash.
- Failed-plan logs: when a batch plan fails after its moves, those moves and their Opportunity Attacks are neither logged nor counted in monster damage. Only a spent Disengage is logged ([Build bonus-action Disengage](closed/099-build-bonus-action-disengage.md)).
- Hit-and-run: a melee creature attacks, then disengages and steps away. It needs a value for distance that range bands lack, and it is the goblins' real edge at the table.
- Pre-roll reactions: a reaction to an attack roll made against the creature before the hit is known, such as Redirect Attack's swap and retarget or a homebrew "impose Disadvantage on the attack" ([Reaction triggers beyond leaving reach and attack rolls](closed/092-reaction-triggers-beyond-reach-and-attack-rolls.md)).
- Turn-start reactions: a trigger for a creature starting its turn near the reactor, with an immunity after a successful save, as in the Chain Devil's Unnerving Gaze ([Reaction triggers beyond leaving reach and attack rolls](closed/092-reaction-triggers-beyond-reach-and-attack-rolls.md)).
- Flat save bonuses on death saves: the rules apply a flat no-ability save bonus to death saves, but the engine doesn't, because reading them would change refolds of encounters saved since 0.1.6. It needs a recorded-version gate like `settings.reaction_windows` ([Dice-valued roll bonuses](closed/093-dice-valued-roll-bonuses.md)).
- Sight-gated targeting: spells and abilities that target "a creature you can see" don't read sight, for darkness or Invisible ([Darkness and Darkvision](closed/091-darkness-and-darkvision.md)).
- Conditions against modifiers: a condition scores once with no horizon, while a modifier with the same effect scores over the buff horizon, so Poisoned (0.25 × threat once) reads below a Disadvantage-on-attacks modifier (0.5 × threat per round). Moving conditions onto the horizon moves baseline cells and needs v5 ([Batch value of debuffs](closed/094-batch-value-of-debuffs.md)).
- Downed bearers and the recast guard: a concentration modifier on a bearer that has dropped still blocks a new concentration cast, for Haste on an ally and for a modifier on an opponent. Changing it changes Haste play on curve inputs ([Batch value of debuffs](closed/094-batch-value-of-debuffs.md)).
- Unscored modifiers: Speed, Reactions, See Invisible, damage-roll and ability-check modifiers score 0 on either side. Reactions, which would deny Opportunity Attacks and Shield, is the first to price ([Batch value of debuffs](closed/094-batch-value-of-debuffs.md)).

## Out of scope

- Non-combat play (story, social, exploration): the tool tests combats and numbers only.
- Items, spells, classes, subclasses, and the prose rules reference: separate future maps. The design should not block them.
- MCP server and long-running server modes: phase 1 is a one-shot CLI. An MCP wrapper can come later over the same commands.
- Agent-written engine code for house rules: phase 1 applies them as prose through overrides. The house rule format leaves room for it.
- Magic items in the standard party: the baseline curve shows no fall-off through level 18, and the High cells at levels 19 and 20 are too thin and too concentrated in a few monsters to change the whole party ([Does the standard party fall off at high levels](closed/052-does-the-standard-party-fall-off-at-high-levels.md)).
- Position-dependent vision: sense ranges measured against range bands, light sources, and who sees whom per pair need positions that abstract engagement doesn't have ([Encounter lighting setting](closed/049-encounter-lighting-setting.md)).
- Engine-played monster turns in live mode (a possible `bench auto-turn` that calls the batch tactics policy): phase 1 has the agent choose every monster action. It can be added over the same step function without changing the live contract.
