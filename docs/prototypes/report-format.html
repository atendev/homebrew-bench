CR comparison and reference facts

Project-derived reading

Difficulty bands
Low: at least 95% party wins and deaths in under 2% of fights. Moderate: at least 75% wins and deaths in under 15%. High: anything riskier. These are Homebrew Bench calibration thresholds.
Effective CR
Lowest whole party level reading Low. Values below level 1 read around 1 or trivial; values above level 20 read over 20.
Baseline
Fixture curve, standard party, batch tactics policy, 100 fights per cell. Rounds and DPR flag anomalies; they do not grade CR.

SRD reference facts

Claimed CR 8
3,900 XP. Monster proficiency bonus: +3.
Party-level context
The SRD budget tables provide Low, Moderate, and High XP budgets per character. This mock does not reproduce the full table.
SRD anchor
The SRD states that one monster with CR equal to the party level is a Low-difficulty fight for four characters.

Wording and coverage findings

  • Agent clause: “The maw may pull a creature into a sealed throat.” The damage is modeled; the sealed-throat outcome is agent judgment.
  • Skipped mechanic: “Ash cloud” is a zone effect not modeled in phase 1. Results may understate the monster’s danger.
  • Inert ability: “Deep-water adaptation” has no combat effect in this encounter.
  • Resolved wording: “Swallow” uses the base-form default for this mock. No alternate form is applied.

The verdict remains available, but skipped mechanics qualify it. An unresolved homebrew ability would block batch execution instead.

Method and limitations

This prototype is a static reading test. A production report would be generated from one batch result, include all 20 party levels, carry input fingerprints, and refuse invalid or unresolved inputs. Thin cells and stale baselines would remain visible warnings rather than silently smoothed values.
